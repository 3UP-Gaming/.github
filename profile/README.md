## Built to launch. Designed to scale.

3UP Gaming is a poker software and iGaming platform provider. For more than nine years our developers, game designers and game architects have built poker rooms for operators who want a secure, fully customizable product instead of a generic template. Today more than 40 in-house specialists support clients around the clock, with active projects across Europe, Asia, Latin America and beyond.

From a single focused poker product to a complete turnkey operation, we give operators the technology, flexibility and support to create memorable player experiences on **web, desktop (Windows and macOS), iOS, Android and Progressive Web Apps**, all running on one game engine, one wallet and one set of player data.

## Security, fair play and compliance

Every product line runs on the same security foundation, because a poker room is only as good as the trust that players and regulators place in it.

- **Certified RNG** technology underpins every table, from cash games to tournaments.
- **Anti-fraud suite** that monitors transactions and account behavior across the platform.
- **Anti-collusion monitoring** with IP tracking to flag collusion and multi-accounting at the same table.
- **24/7 system monitoring**, with the same team providing around-the-clock post-sale support.
- **Data encryption** for player data and financial transactions, whether payments are fiat or crypto.
- **Compliance-ready foundations** that support the licensing requirements of the markets you choose to operate in.

> **Note on licensing.** 3UP Gaming provides software. Operators are responsible for holding the licenses required in their jurisdictions and for complying with local gambling, advertising and age-verification laws. White Label is built for operators who already hold a gaming license; Turnkey and Standalone are the better starting points if you do not have one yet.

Found a security issue? Please write to [contactus@3upgaming.com](mailto:contactus@3upgaming.com?subject=Security%20report) with the subject line "Security report" and do not disclose it publicly before we have had the chance to respond.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>What is the difference between Turnkey, White Label and Standalone?</b></summary>

<br>

Turnkey and White Label both give you a fully working, ready-to-brand poker room on 3UP technology, so you launch in weeks instead of years. Standalone goes a step further: it is your own independent room with no shared network, no revenue share with other brands and full control over rake and rules. Poker Skin sits at the other end: a branded room that shares liquidity and infrastructure with a larger network, usually the fastest and lowest-cost way to launch.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I buy the software outright, or only rent it?</b></summary>

<br>

Both. We offer customized licensing agreements for full ownership and a rental-based model.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which cryptocurrencies and wallets are supported?</b></summary>

<br>

Crypto Poker supports TON, Bitcoin (BTC), USDT and Ethereum (ETH), with wallet connections through MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Exodus and Tonkeeper. Crypto can also be layered onto White Label, Turnkey and Standalone packages next to standard fiat payments.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How is fairness guaranteed?</b></summary>

<br>

Every product line runs on certified random number generator (RNG) technology, paired with an anti-fraud suite, IP-based anti-collusion monitoring, 24/7 system monitoring and end-to-end data encryption.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is the platform mobile-friendly?</b></summary>

Yes. All product lines are built for iOS, Android and Progressive Web Apps, with responsive design for phones, tablets, laptops and desktops, and real-time sync so a player can switch devices mid-session without losing a seat, a stack or a hand in progress.

</details>
